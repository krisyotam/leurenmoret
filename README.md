# Leuren Moret: Global Nuclear Coverup — Site Mirror

A complete preservation of **leurenmoret.info**, the assembled works of Leuren Moret (BS, MS, PhD ABD) — geoscientist, nuclear whistleblower, and independent radiation researcher. The original site went offline and this repository serves as the canonical archive.

---

## About Leuren Moret

Leuren Moret is a geoscientist who worked at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and UC Berkeley's Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. She became a leading voice on the health and environmental consequences of depleted uranium (DU) weapons, nuclear power plant radiation contamination, directed energy weapons, and the broader geopolitical machinery that suppresses this research.

Her work spans:
- **Depleted uranium** — DU weapons, battlefield contamination, Gulf War Syndrome, long-term genetic damage
- **Fukushima** — fallout mapping, Pacific Ocean contamination, cover-up analysis
- **HAARP / RFDE** — electromagnetic and directed-energy weapons research
- **Geopolitics** — NATO, Jesuit influence, BRICS/Silk Road dynamics, false flags
- **Radiation ecology** — environmental and population-level effects of ionizing radiation

## links.json

`links.json` at the repository root catalogs every external URL cited across the site — grouped by domain. Useful for understanding her primary sources, which organizations she referenced, and what external material has since disappeared.

## Original Technology

The site was generated by **Sandvox 2.10.x** (a Mac desktop publishing application) using the "Clockwork Blue" theme. HTML is hand-organized but machine-generated with heavy inline styling.

## Status

This repository is a faithful mirror. No content has been altered or removed. The `links.json` documents what sources she cited. A modern Next.js 16 static rebuild (`leurenmoret-modern`) preserves the structure and content in a typesafe, long-term maintainable format.
